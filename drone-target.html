Overview

YOLO-based detection paired with a classifier workflow to distinguish valid targets from non-targets and background in aerial imagery/video. This page is meant to hold detailed methods, dataset notes, and demo results.

Key highlights

  • Real-time detection pipeline (segmentation/detection front-end).
  • Coarse “valid/invalid” classification stage for decision-making.
  • Designed for practical deployment with OpenCV / video streams.
